Privacy Policy

Wear OS Watch Face Application — GoodTimes Initiative

Last updated: February, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GoodTimes Initiative (“Developer”, “we”, “us”) handles information in relation to the Wear OS watch face application (“the Application”).

1. Data Collection

The Application does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data.

  • No personal or identifiable information is collected
  • No data is stored on external servers
  • No analytics, tracking, profiling, or logging mechanisms are used
  • No third-party services are integrated for data collection

All displayed information (including time, date, weather, heart rate, steps, step goal progress, UV index, chance of rain, and battery level) is retrieved locally on the user’s device through standard Wear OS system APIs.

2. Permissions

The Application may request system permissions (such as access to sensors or location) solely to display relevant information on the watch face.

These permissions are:

  • Used only for on-device functionality
  • Never used to collect, store, or transmit data
  • Never shared with third parties

3. Data Sharing

The Developer does not share any data with third parties, as no user data is collected or stored.

4. Data Security

Since the Application does not collect or retain data, there is no risk of data exposure, misuse, or unauthorized access related to user information.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal data from any users, including children under the age of 13.
